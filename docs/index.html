The problem

Extreme heat measurably increases emergency department demand, but hospitals largely react after the surge hits rather than preparing for it. This project builds the data-driven bridge between a climate forecast and a staffing decision.

How it works

Four stages, from raw climate signal to a hospital-level recommendation.

01 / CLIMATE

Heat exposure

Daily temperature, heatwave days and severity by region (Météo-France, Copernicus).

02 / DEMAND

ED demand model

Historical heat-to-ED-visit relationship (SurSaUD/OSCOUR iCanicule indicator).

03 / CAPACITY

Resource gap

Expected demand compared against baseline nurse, doctor and bed capacity (Eurostat).

04 / DECISION

Recommendation

A plain-language staffing and bed recommendation for the administrator.
